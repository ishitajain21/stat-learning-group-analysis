The dataset that we examine is tracking legal decisions made by either courts or tribunals on the topic of generative AI use in the legal process. Specifically, these cases concern AI hallucination of laws, quotes, or other instances of falsely generated material. We are investigating how the number of such cases has changed over time. 

First, our investigation includes a preliminary exploration of the relationship between the number of cases and specialty (e.g., judge, lawyer, litigant), as well as the number of cases and the subspecialty of law (e.g, contract, tort, tax). We also examine the breakdown between cases that result in professional sanctions and those that do not. These three topics constitute our three data visualizations. 

Next, we use regression to investigate the relationship between time (across the months included in the dataset) and the number of cases. We ask want to know if there is there a trend across time in the number of cases. More specifically, we ask: has the number of documented AI hallucination court cases increased over time? 

Our first statistical analysis is a linear regression model.
